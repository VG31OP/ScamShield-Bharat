# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Indian citizens, smartphone users, families, and elderly citizens encountering unsolicited or suspicious SMS messages, WhatsApp forwards, emails, and banking links. Secondary users include student developers, researchers, and cyber safety advocates evaluating heuristic fraud detection.

## Product Purpose
ScamShield Bharat (भारत का डिजिटल सुरक्षा कवच) is a citizen-first, AI-assisted cyber scam and fraud detection platform. It enables Indian citizens to paste suspicious messages or URLs and receive an immediate, 100% explainable 0–100 risk assessment with plain-language warning signs and recommended immediate actions in English, Hindi, and Gujarati.

## Positioning
Unlike opaque LLM chatbots or expensive enterprise antivirus software, ScamShield Bharat delivers deterministic, transparent heuristic rules with zero data storage, zero paid API dependencies, and direct integration with India's 1930 National Cybercrime Helpline and CFCFRMS framework.

## Operating Context
Everyday mobile and desktop web browsing in India, where rapid digital payment adoption (UPI, Netbanking) is frequently targeted by urgency-driven fraud (fake KYC lockouts, 9:30 PM electricity cutoff notices, "Digital Arrest" extortion, and fake courier delivery SMS).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Multilingual detection and explanations in English, Hindi (हिन्दी), and Gujarati (ગુજરાતી).
- Explainable composite risk score (0–100) across Low, Medium, High, and Critical tiers.
- Threat vector quantification across Urgency, Impersonation, Credential Theft, Financial Traps, Remote Access, and Deceptive Links.
- Structural URL safety and typosquatting inspection without executing or visiting untrusted links.
- Interactive incident response triage wizard with the crucial 2-hour Golden Hours window.
- 100% client-side execution with zero message retention or tracking.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: ScamShield Bharat (भारत का डिजिटल सुरक्षा कवच) 🇮🇳
- Identity: Authoritative, civic-tech cybersecurity service.
- Tone: Practical, vigilant, calming, and decisive.

## Evidence on Hand
- Curated rule set reflecting advisories by the Indian Cybercrime Coordination Centre (I4C), CERT-In, and Reserve Bank of India (RBI).
- 13 automated engine test cases validating ReDoS resilience, zero-width Unicode sanitization, malformed URLs, and regional Indian scripts.

## Product Principles
1. **Explainable and Auditable**: Every warning displays points contributed and the exact rationale.
2. **Privacy by Design**: Sensitive messages and credentials never leave the user's browser.
3. **Vernacular First**: Full parity across English, Hindi, and Gujarati for equitable digital protection.
4. **Action-Oriented Response**: Moves beyond fear to provide immediate steps, evidence preservation guidelines, and 1930 helpline routing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard navigable controls, visible focus rings (`focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-[#074ce0]`), WCAG AAA compliant text contrast, semantic HTML, and reduced-motion support.
